# PCI DSS 10.x / SOC2 logging vs. deletion rights over IPs and access records

Legitimate interest usually carries this, but the tension is permanent.

## Instruments
- mandating: PCI DSS PCI DSS v4.0.1, Req. 10.2.2, 10.5.1 (https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/)
- context: SOC 2 AICPA 2017 Trust Services Criteria, CC7.2 (points of focus) (https://www.aicpa.org/content/dam/aicpa/interestareas/frc/assuranceadvisoryservices/downloadabledocuments/trust-services-criteria.pdf)
- conflicting: GDPR Art. 17(1); exception at Art. 17(3)(b) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
Review: SOC 2 doesn't require logging IPs, and PCI DSS is contractual. Supports the 'fails' verdict. Review by claude-opus-5-5, 2026-10-31, countersigned by Will Foti, 2026-11-06.
