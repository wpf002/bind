# GDPR Art. 17 erasure vs. append-only ledgers

Erasure requires the operation the threat model forbids.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Art. 17(1); exception at Art. 17(3)(b) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
Review: EDPB Guidelines 02/2025 (draft) say encrypted data stays personal data, so key destruction isn't erasure. Strengthens the case. Final text not yet checked. Review by claude-opus-5-5, 2026-10-31, pending human countersign.

## Inclusion
Partially, but only by moving personal data out of the architecture, i.e. by not using the property you adopted the ledger for.
