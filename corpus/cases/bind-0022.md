# Highly effective age assurance without identification

UK OSA s. 81 requires age checks that are highly effective at determining whether a user is a child; doing that without learning who the user is has no deployed construction.

## Instruments
- mandating: UK OSA s. 81 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/81)

## Analysis
Anonymous credentials and zero-knowledge attribute proofs give the right shape of answer: prove over-18 without revealing identity. In deployment the issuer learns the claim, the relying party learns the result, and collusion between them relinks the user. Nothing published closes that gap at the required effectiveness while keeping the unlinkability property.

## Inclusion
Primitives exist; a deployable construction at the mandated effectiveness does not. That is a research gap, not an impossibility, and it is the only category from which engineering resolution can arrive.
