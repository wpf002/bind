# EU data retention mandates vs. ePrivacy Art. 15(1)

Member states keep enacting general and indiscriminate retention of traffic and location data, and the CJEU keeps holding that ePrivacy Art. 15(1), read with the Charter, precludes it (Tele2, La Quadrature, SpaceNet). Targeted retention and general retention of source IP addresses survive, so the conflict sits at that boundary. Structural, cyclical.

## Instruments
- mandating: EU Retention Directive 2002/58/EC Art. 15(1) (enabling clause); national retention statutes (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32002L0058)
- conflicting: ePrivacy Art. 15(1) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32002L0058)
- context: CJEU C-293/12 & C-594/12; C-203/15 & C-698/15; C-511/18, C-512/18 & C-520/18; C-140/20; C-793/19 & C-794/19 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62018CJ0511)

## Analysis
Digital Rights Ireland invalidated the EU Retention Directive itself. The later rulings struck national regimes.
