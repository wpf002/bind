# UK Online Safety Act s.121 scanning notices vs. E2EE

Same shape, different direction: the law requires detection capability that the architecture exists to preclude.

## Instruments
- mandating: UK OSA s.121(2)(a)(iii) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/121)

## Analysis
Deferred in practice ("when technically feasible"), which is a legislature admitting the incompatibility without repealing the clause.

Review: 'When technically feasible' isn't in OSA s.121. It's a ministerial assurance (HL Deb 6 Sept 2023, col 458), now cited. The analysis calls it the legislature's admission; it's the Government's, and Lord Moylan said in the same debate that the clause text doesn't contain it.
