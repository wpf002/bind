# Swiss Art. 271 against compelled US document production

Swiss Criminal Code Art. 271 criminalises acting on Swiss territory for a foreign state without authorisation; a US subpoena requires exactly that collection.

## Instruments
- mandating: StGB Art. 271 Art. 271 (https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/54/757_781_799/en#art_271)
- mandating: FRCP 34 Rule 34(a) (https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_34)

## Analysis
Gathering documents in Switzerland to satisfy a US order has been treated as an unauthorised act for a foreign authority. The Swiss entity cannot lawfully perform the collection and the US court holds it in control of the records. Authorisation exists as a procedure but is granted narrowly and slowly relative to discovery deadlines.

## Inclusion
The prohibited act is the collection itself. No storage architecture makes collection-by-the-Swiss-entity something other than an act on Swiss territory.
