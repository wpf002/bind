# Continuous real-time data access without a named access construct

Data Act Art. 5(1) requires readily available product data be made accessible to a third party continuously and in real time where applicable; no standard defines what construct delivers that for a device whose data path terminates in a key the data holder does not control.

## Instruments
- mandating: Data Act Art. 5(1) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj)

## Analysis
The provision describes a capability in operational terms and leaves the mechanism unspecified. For architectures where the holder cannot decrypt the stream, there is nothing to make continuously accessible without first changing the key arrangement, which the Article does not address. This is a drafting gap rather than an incompatibility, and it is listed here so it cannot be counted as one.

## Inclusion
A construction may exist once someone specifies it. It is not merely expensive and not known to be impossible, which is the second reason resolvability has three values rather than two.
