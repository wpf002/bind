# Schrems II transfer restrictions vs. CLOUD Act production orders

A US warrant reaches data in a provider's control wherever it's stored (18 U.S.C. § 2713). GDPR Art. 48 bars that warrant from serving as the transfer ground unless an international agreement backs it, so the provider must find another Chapter V ground or refuse. Same data, same provider.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Art. 48 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)
- mandating: CLOUD Act 18 U.S.C. § 2713 (CLOUD Act § 103(a)(1), Pub. L. 115-141), applied through § 2703 warrants (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2713)
- context: Schrems II C-311/18 (Schrems II); GDPR Arts. 46, 48 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62018CJ0311)
