# FATF Travel Rule vs. self-hosted wallets

The rule requires originator/beneficiary information to accompany the transfer. A self-hosted wallet has no institution to attach it to and no counterparty to receive it.

## Instruments
- mandating: FATF FATF Recommendation 16 (renamed 'Payment transparency', June 2025) and INR.15 para 7(b), fn 47 (https://www.fatf-gafi.org/content/dam/fatf-gafi/recommendations/FATF%20Recommendations%202012.pdf)

## Analysis
Review: The Travel Rule binds VASPs and financial institutions only, and the data needn't travel on-chain. Self-hosted to self-hosted transfers carry no obligation. Fails inclusion. Review by claude-opus-5-5, 2026-10-31, countersigned by Will Foti, 2026-11-06.

Amended from v0.1 by claude-opus-5-5, 2026-10-31. Signed off by Will Foti, 2026-11-01, on claude-opus-5-5's source verification.
inclusionReasoning: "No, for self-hosted endpoints specifically." The source contradicts it. Reworded to match the countersigned review, 2026-11-06.

## Inclusion
Yes. The Travel Rule binds VASPs and financial institutions only, and footnote 47 says the information need not travel with the transfer. A transfer between two self-hosted wallets has no obliged party, so the rule asks nothing of it. Kept as a boundary case.
