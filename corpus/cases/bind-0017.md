# Four-day incident disclosure during active containment

SEC Item 1.05 requires public disclosure within four business days of a materiality determination; disclosing the incident while the intruder retains access tells the intruder what is known.

## Instruments
- mandating: SEC Item 1.05 Item 1.05 (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/section-229.106)

## Analysis
The filing is public and immediate. During containment the defender's advantage is the attacker's uncertainty about detection, so the filing spends that advantage on the deadline. The Attorney General delay provision exists and is narrow and slow, which makes the sequencing available in principle and unreliable in practice.

## Inclusion
A firm can disclose on time and accept the operational cost, so this is not impossible. It is retained because the cost falls on the security outcome the other duty protects.
