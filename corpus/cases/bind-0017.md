# "Anonymous" as a binary vs. re-identifiability as a continuum

GDPR treats anonymized data as out of scope. Re-identifiability is a function of dataset sparsity plus available auxiliary data, and auxiliary data grows monotonically over time.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Recital 26 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
Consequence: a dataset can become personal data years later without anyone touching it. No engineering action at time T fixes this.
