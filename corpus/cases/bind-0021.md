# AI Act Art. 10(5) bias testing vs. GDPR Art. 9

Testing for bias against protected classes requires processing special-category data that Art. 9 prohibits.

## Instruments
- mandating: AI Act Art. 10(5) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689)
- conflicting: GDPR Art. 9(1) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
It was resolved by an explicit statutory carve-out. It's the corpus's only worked example of what resolution actually looks like: a legislature patching the conflict, not an engineer.
