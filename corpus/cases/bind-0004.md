# Erasure duty against non-rewriteable broker-dealer retention

SEC 17a-4(f) requires records be kept in a non-erasable format for years; GDPR Art. 17 requires erasure of the personal data inside those records on request.

## Instruments
- mandating: SEC 17a-4 § 240.17a-4(f) (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/section-240.17a-4)
- mandating: GDPR Art. 17(1) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)

## Analysis
Both duties attach to the same firm for the same records. The WORM requirement is satisfied precisely by the medium's inability to delete, so a firm that can comply with the erasure request has already failed the retention rule. The legal-obligation exemption in Art. 17(3)(b) covers obligations under Union or Member State law, not US federal rules, which is where the conflict bites for dual-registered firms.

## Inclusion
The exemption that would resolve it is drafted by jurisdiction, not by subject matter. No engineering choice changes which legislature wrote the retention rule.
