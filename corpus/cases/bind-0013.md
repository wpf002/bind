# Detection orders against encrypted interpersonal communications

The proposed CSA Regulation lets authorities order detection of abuse material in interpersonal communications; the services named operate so that no server-side party can read content.

## Instruments
- mandating: CSA Regulation Arts. 7–10 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52022PC0209)

## Analysis
The proposal's text does not mandate breaking encryption and does not exempt encrypted services either, which leaves the order addressed to a party that cannot execute it. Every published route to execution moves inspection onto the client. The shape is the same as the OSA case and the instrument is different, which is what makes it a class rather than a single controversy.

## Inclusion
The order presumes server-side knowledge of content. The architecture's purpose is that no server-side party has it.
