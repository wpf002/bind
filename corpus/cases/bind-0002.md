# Erasure/deletion rights vs. mandatory retention

GDPR Art. 17(3)(b) exempts retention only where Union or Member State law requires it. A broker-dealer under both the GDPR and SEC Rule 17a-4 must keep records that a US rule requires, so an erasure request and the retention duty command opposite state changes on the same records. The architecture didn't cause this and can't fix it.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Art. 17(1); exception at Art. 17(3)(b) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)
- conflicting: SEC 17 CFR 240.17a-4(b), (f)(2)(i) (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.17a-4)
