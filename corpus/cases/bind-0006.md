# CLOUD Act production order against the Art. 48 disclosure bar

18 U.S.C. § 2713 compels a US-reachable provider to disclose data wherever stored; GDPR Art. 48 bars recognition of that order absent an international agreement.

## Instruments
- mandating: CLOUD Act § 2713 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2713)
- mandating: GDPR Art. 48 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)

## Analysis
The provider holds the data and is subject to both. Complying with the warrant is an unlawful transfer; refusing is contempt. The conflict is structural rather than situational because it follows from corporate reachability, and reachability is what makes the provider useful to its EU customers in the first place. Two mandating instruments, which is why the schema cannot model one instrument per case.

## Inclusion
Sovereign cloud structures reduce exposure but do not remove it where any entity in the group is US-reachable, and the group structure is a legal fact, not an engineering parameter.
