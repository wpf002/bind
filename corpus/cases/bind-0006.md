# eIDAS 2.0 QWAC trust-root mandate vs. root program distrust authority

Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 requires browsers to recognise QWACs (Art. 45(1a)) but leaves them free to secure TLS as they choose (Recital 65) and to take precautionary measures against a certificate (Art. 45a(2)). The draft's non-removable trust root wasn't enacted.

## Instruments
- mandating: eIDAS 2.0 Art. 45(1a), Art. 45a (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1183)

## Analysis
Resolved legislatively between draft and adoption. Art. 45a(2) measures require notification and remain subject to supervisory review, so a contested distrust hasn't been tested.
