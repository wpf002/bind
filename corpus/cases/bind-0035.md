# DSA monthly active recipients vs. no individual tracking

Platforms must publish average monthly active recipients counting unique recipients once, and aren't required to track individuals.

## Instruments
- mandating: DSA Recital 77 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065)
- conflicting: GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
A count of unique recipients across devices and logged-out sessions needs a stable identifier per person. Without tracking, the number is an estimate whose error depends on the deduplication heuristic. The 45 million VLOP threshold rides on that estimate. Recital 77 resolves it by text: count once "where possible" and no tracking required. That is a legislative patch, as in G5.

## Inclusion
An exact unique-recipient count without per-person tracking is impossible for logged-out and multi-device use. The legislature accepted an approximation, which is the resolution. Passes on the same basis as G5.
