# Age verification mandates vs. GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)

Verifying age requires collecting identity evidence; minimization forbids collecting more than necessary for the purpose.

## Instruments
- mandating: Age Verification Online Safety Act 2023 (UK), s.81(2)-(3) (Part 5) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/81)
- conflicting: GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis


## Inclusion
Yes, in principle: ZK age attestation. Not deployed at scale. This is a technology gap, not a true incompatibility, and belongs in a different bucket than D.
