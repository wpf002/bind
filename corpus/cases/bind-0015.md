# Access requests against pseudonymous-by-design accounts

GDPR Art. 15 requires a controller to confirm and disclose processing to the data subject; a system designed to hold no identifying attributes cannot authenticate who the requester is.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Art. 15(1) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)
- context: GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)

## Analysis
Answering the request correctly requires linking a claimant to records. The design holds nothing that supports that link, so the controller either refuses a valid request or starts collecting identity documents to service it. Collecting them makes every account identifiable in order to honour a right meant to protect identifiable people.

## Inclusion
Art. 11 contemplates controllers unable to identify the subject, which acknowledges the gap rather than closing it. Any mechanism that closes it destroys the property.
