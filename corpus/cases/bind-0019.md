# Twenty-four-hour early warning against investigative non-disclosure

NIS2 Art. 23(4) requires an early warning within 24 hours of awareness; law-enforcement non-disclosure instructions during an active investigation forbid the same communication.

## Instruments
- mandating: NIS2 Art. 23(4) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj)

## Analysis
Both duties bind the same operator within the same window. The authority receiving the warning and the authority running the investigation are different bodies with different mandates, and the coordination that would sequence them is not a condition of either duty. The operator chooses which instruction to follow.

## Inclusion
Coordination is possible where the two authorities are willing. Retained because the duty binds the operator regardless of whether they coordinate, and the operator cannot compel them to.
