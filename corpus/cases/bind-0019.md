# Art. 15 / Art. 22 explainability vs. model opacity + trade secret

Art. 15(1)(h), as read in C-203/22, owes the data subject the procedure and principles actually applied to their data, and a complex formula doesn't satisfy it. Post-hoc attributions (SHAP, LIME) approximate the model's output. They aren't the procedure applied, and the procedure applied is weights.

## Instruments
- mandating: GDPR Art. 15(1)(h) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)
- context: GDPR Art. 22(1), 22(3); Recital 71 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)
- context: Trade Secret Directive (EU) 2016/943, Art. 2(1); GDPR Recital 63 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

## Analysis
C-203/22 sends trade-secret claims to the supervisory authority or court for balancing, so trade secret is a limit, not a bar. Art. 22(3) asks for human intervention and contest. The explanation language is in Recital 71, which isn't binding.
