# Russia unified app store mandate vs. single-root iOS distribution

Since 1 September 2025 Russian consumer law bars any condition on smartphones and tablets that restricts installing apps through the unified app store, or the functions, updates and payments of those apps. iOS installs only code the platform vendor has reviewed and signed.

## Instruments
- mandating: Consumer Rights Law Art. 4(4.2), inserted by Federal Law No. 194-FZ of 07.07.2025 (https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_305/030ee6c590df5a57ef7c36c7cc5c6a59e5373ecc/)
- mandating: 194-FZ Art. 1(1)-(2), Art. 2 (http://www.kremlin.ru/acts/bank/52093)

## Analysis
Art. 4(4.1) puts a unified app store among the programs that listed devices must carry pre-installed. Government Decree No. 1867 lists smartphones and tablets. Art. 4(4.2), added by 194-FZ, removes the platform's controls over that store. No condition may restrict installing programs through it, restrict search, updates, settings or notifications for its apps, set special pricing terms, or limit payment methods. 194-FZ also amended the preamble, so a device that fails 4.1 or 4.2 is a defective good under the law. The iOS security model has one root: the vendor reviews and signs everything that runs. A second store that installs and updates code on its own decision is a second root. A vendor review or signing step that can refuse one of that store's apps is a condition restricting installation, which sub-item 1 bars. The three sub-items carry no exception for security or integrity measures.

## Inclusion
No. Single-root distribution means one party decides what installs. Art. 4(4.2)(1) bars any condition that restricts installation through the unified store, so the vendor can't keep its review as a gate. Notarization, capability limits and payment rules are each a restriction the sub-items name. Spending doesn't help, because the conflict is over who holds the root. A vendor keeps the property only on devices that aren't sold in Russia.
