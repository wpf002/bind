# PRC export approval against US discovery obligations

PRC Data Security Law Art. 36 forbids providing data to a foreign judicial body without approval; FRCP 34 requires production of everything in the party's control.

## Instruments
- mandating: PRC DSL Art. 36 (https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202106/t20210610_311888.html)
- mandating: FRCP 34 Rule 34(a) (https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_34)

## Analysis
A party with PRC operations holds responsive data under both regimes. Approval is discretionary and in practice slow or unavailable. US courts apply comity analysis but have repeatedly ordered production anyway, so the party chooses which law to break. Data localisation does not help because control, not location, triggers the discovery duty.

## Inclusion
No architecture removes 'control' in the Rule 34 sense while the party continues to operate the systems; divesting operations is not an engineering remedy.
