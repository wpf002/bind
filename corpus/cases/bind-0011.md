# Right to repair vs. parts pairing / attested firmware

Secure boot and component attestation work by rejecting unattested parts. Repair requires installing exactly those.

## Instruments
- mandating: EU R2R Art. 5(6) (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024L1799)
- mandating: US States Or. SB 1596 (2024), Or. Laws 2024 ch. 69, § 1(2)(b) (https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2024R1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/SB1596/Enrolled)

## Analysis
Review: Directive 2024/1799 Art. 5(6) exempts techniques 'justified by legitimate and objective factors'. If security attestation qualifies, the directive resolves the conflict. Verdict held pending that reading. Review by claude-opus-5-5, 2026-10-31, pending human countersign.

## Inclusion
No, not without a plural attestation model that doesn't currently exist.
