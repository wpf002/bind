# UK IPA technical capability notices vs. provider-blind E2EE backup

A TCN can require an operator to be able to remove encryption it applied. In provider-blind E2EE the operator holds no key.

## Instruments
- mandating: IPA 2016 s. 253(5)(c) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/section/253)
- mandating: IPA 2016 s. 257(3A) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/25/section/257)

## Analysis
s. 253(5)(c) covers removal of "electronic protection applied by or on behalf of that operator". In an E2EE backup design the operator applies the protection but keys stay on user devices. The only way to have the capability is to hold or escrow a key, which removes the property. s. 257(3A), added in 2024, then forbids the operator from shipping changes that reduce capability while a notice is under review, so the operator can't add E2EE in the meantime. s. 253(8) reaches operators outside the UK. A provider serving UK users can comply only by not offering provider-blind encryption to them.

## Inclusion
No key arrangement gives the operator a removal capability while keeping it unable to decrypt. Splitting keys among parties only moves the capability. The operator's options are compliance by key escrow or withdrawal of the feature.
